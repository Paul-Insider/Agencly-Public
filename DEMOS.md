# Explore the current demos

AGENCLY covers white-label applications across business sectors and a partner-agency model. The two public demos currently available are Property experiences: distinct residence identities sharing a common guest experience concept. Each can be opened directly in a browser.

See the [platform overview](README.md) for the broader sector scope and [partner agencies](PARTNERS.md) for the white-label agency offering. Future sector demos will be added as they become available.

## MayaLuxHome

A Caribbean residence experience, with accommodation imagery, a gallery, local discovery and concierge presentation.

- [Open MayaLuxHome](https://mayaluxhome.pages.dev/)
- [Installation and QR code](https://mayaluxhome.pages.dev/download/)

## DAR OLIVA

A Marrakech residence experience, bringing its own olive and cream identity to spaces, local recommendations and guest services.

- [Open DAR OLIVA](https://daroliva.pages.dev/)
- [Installation and QR code](https://daroliva.pages.dev/download/)

## What to explore

- Home and the visual identity of each residence.
- Gallery and photographs of the spaces.
- Living Here and Explore for the surrounding destination.
- Concierge and Contact presentation.
- The installation page for the available web installation guidance and QR code.

These releases are web/PWA demonstrations. Installation depends on the device and browser. Native macOS, Windows, Android and iOS binaries are not included in these releases.

Demo interactions and owner screens are for evaluation. Do not treat them as a live reservation, payment or operational property service. Refer to each demo's legal page for its demonstration terms.
