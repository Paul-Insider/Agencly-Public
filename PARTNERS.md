# Build your white-label agency with AGENCLY

AGENCLY's partner model is for agencies, developers and independent entrepreneurs who want to offer branded applications under their own business identity. It extends across application sectors, including eCommerce, restaurants, courses, travel and Property.

## Two layers of white label

| Layer | Identity and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Your agency | A customer-facing storefront, visual direction and branded sales materials shaped around your business |
| Your clients' applications | Applications presented under each client's own identity, with the functionality agreed for their business |

The agreed storefront, app interfaces and sales materials can be presented without visible AGENCLY branding. The partnership agreement identifies the exact surfaces, product rights and operating arrangements covered.

## An offering shaped around your market

A partnership can cover monthly application subscriptions, app acquisition or an agreed combination of both. The selected sectors, application models, adaptations and customer journeys are defined together.

A multi-sector catalogue gives an agency a broader direction than a single vertical. The models available for a specific partnership depend on their documented availability and qualification status. The current public demos cover Property; they are examples of application identities, not the full limit of the agency model.

## Define the operating framework together

The partnership discussion should establish:

- The agency identity, target audience and customer-facing presentation.
- The application sectors, models and functionality within scope.
- Product rights, branding rights and any acquisition deliverables.
- Customer contracting, subscriptions, payments and operating costs.
- Configuration, fulfilment, launch responsibilities and client support.
- Commercial schedules and the responsibilities retained by each party.

No public rate card or earnings commitment is made by this page. Definitive terms belong in the written partnership agreement.

## Start with a conversation

An initial enquiry introduces your agency, audience and application goals. If the direction aligns, the non-disclosure agreement process precedes access to the detailed commercial brief. An enquiry does not itself sign an agreement, grant confidential access or activate a partnership.
